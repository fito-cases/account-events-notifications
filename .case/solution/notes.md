# Intended approach (evaluator only)

The local worked solution is commit `091249f` on `case/solution-v1.0.0`; the starter is commit `cc43555` on `main`. The source and tests are identical across those refs except for `src/main/java/account/CurrentAccount.java`. On the starter, both accepted withdrawal and incoming paths update the balance and then reach an empty `afterSuccessfulTransaction()` extension point. On the solution, accepted paths publish events after writing the balance; rejected paths never publish. The account represents money in `long` cents, rejects nonpositive amounts, catches arithmetic overflow, and allows the inclusive negative-limit bound.

On the solution, `CurrentAccount` keeps an `EnumMap<AccountEventType, List<AccountEventListener>>` for per-event subscriptions. `subscribe` and `unsubscribe` change the recipient list; `publish` snapshots the list and delivers immutable `AccountEvent` context to every listener. On withdrawal it compares the previous and new balances for overdraft (nonnegative to negative), and tests the individual withdrawal amount for the R$5,000–under-R$10,000 and R$10,000-or-more bands; the overdraft and band alerts can both fire. On incoming activity it compares the previous and new balances for crossing R$50,000 from below. `AccountNotificationRouting` composes owner, account manager, digital security, and investment management roles outside the account. `AccountEventRecipient` uses a `MessageDelivery` abstraction; `SmsDelivery` and `WhatsAppDelivery` independently capture messages rendered with event name, customer name/CPF, branch, account number and completed balance. No network service or credentials are needed.

# Rubric evidence

- **observer-collaboration:** inspect the per-event subscriber map, `subscribe`/`unsubscribe`, shared `onAccountEvent` callback and one-to-many dispatch; the multiple-recipient/removal checks in `AccountEventsTest.testRecipientThresholdBoundaries` exercise this.
- **sender-independence:** `CurrentAccount.java` has no SMS, WhatsApp or sender imports/fields/references; routing and delivery are separate classes. `grep -nE 'Sms|WhatsApp|Sender' src/main/java/account/CurrentAccount.java` yielded no matches on the solution branch.
- **event-recipients:** `AccountEventsTest.testRecipientThresholdBoundaries` covers all four withdrawal origins and three incoming origins, exact R$5,000/R$10,000/R$50,000 boundaries and adjacent amounts, overdraft crossing versus remaining negative, role routing, nonrecipients and subscription removal.
- **notification-context:** `AccountEventsTest.testNotificationContentAndCompletedBalance` checks captured SMS and WhatsApp messages for event name, customer name, CPF, branch, account number and R$50,000.00 post-transaction balance.
- **completed-state:** the same test observes the account's balance from inside the synchronous callback and checks that an event carries it; `CurrentAccountBaselineTest` covers rejected transactions and unchanged balances with no emission.
